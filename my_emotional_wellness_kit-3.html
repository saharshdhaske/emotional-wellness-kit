❤️ My Emotional Wellness Kit ❤️

A little kit to help me understand my feelings and feel calm and happy.

Things that will go in the KitHow to Use
Stress ballSqueeze it gently when you feel angry, worried or stressed.
Teddy bear or soft toyGive it a hug when you feel sad, lonely or upset.
Colouring book and crayonsColour a picture quietly to relax and feel happy.
Emotion cardsChoose a card that shows how you feel and talk about your feeling with someone you trust.
Remember: All feelings are okay. If a feeling is too big or does not go away, talk to a trusted adult.
